## Description [4000]
The storm took your boat. The island will take the rest, unless you learn its ways.

Castaway is a first-person survival adventure on a hand-crafted island. Wash up beside the wreck with a crate of supplies and nothing else, then build your way from bare hands to a home, a raft and a rescue.

SURVIVE
• Keep hunger, thirst and warmth up as days turn to night and summer turns to a hard winter.
• Light campfires to cook, stay warm and keep the wolves at bay.
• Sleep in a lean-to, forage berries and mushrooms, and learn which red caps make you sick.

CRAFT AND BUILD
• Knap a stone axe and pickaxe, then forge iron, obsidian and cobalt tools.
• Build a home piece by piece: floors, walls, a door, a roof, storage chests and a garden.
• Spearfish in the shallows and grow food through the seasons.

EXPLORE
• Climb to waterfalls, stone circles and a lookout tower.
• Descend into a mountain cave of crystal grottos and black water.
• Read messages in bottles, follow a treasure map and spot distant islands through a spyglass.
• Sail a raft to a volcanic isle and a lighthouse on the horizon.

A LIVING ISLAND
• Deer, hares, boars, crabs, foxes and wolves, each with its own behavior.
• Earn a fox's trust and Pip will follow you, sit by your fire and warn you about wolves.
• Storms roll in off the sea, lightning sets trees alight, and snow covers the island in winter.
• Every sound and note of music is generated live as you play.

MADE FOR TOUCH
• A floating thumbstick, hold-to-strike, a context-aware Use button and gentle aim assist.
• Adjustable look speed, button size and haptics.
• Plays fully offline. No ads, no account, no tracking.

## App Review notes
Castaway is a single-player game that runs entirely offline; there is no account or login. To reach gameplay quickly, tap Begin, then Skip intro. Tap the supply chest on the beach (Use button) to collect starting supplies.
