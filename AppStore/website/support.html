Castaway: Survival Island – Support

Need help or found a bug? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL and include your device model and what happened.

Quick tips

  • Moving: put your left thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen. Push the stick to its edge to sprint.
  • Looking: drag on the right side of the screen.
  • Striking: hold the Strike button to keep swinging.
  • Using things: the Use button lights up and shows what it will do.
  • Game running slowly? Open Pause → Settings, set Graphics to Low and keep Smooth frame rate on.
  • Your progress saves automatically every few seconds and whenever you leave the app.
