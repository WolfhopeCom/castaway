Castaway: Survival Island – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Castaway: Survival Island does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your saved game and your settings (such as look speed and graphics quality) are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, shows no advertising, contains no analytics or third-party tracking, and does not connect to the internet while you play.

Children

Because no information is collected from anyone, no information is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
